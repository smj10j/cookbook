pitch: >-
  This is the Tuesday-night salmon that eats like you fussed. A fifty-fifty rub
  of brown sugar and paprika gets pressed hard into the top of each fillet, where
  it melts and caramelizes in the oven into a burnished, faintly smoky crust —
  the sugar lacquering, the paprika going deep red, a whisper of red pepper flakes
  behind it. Everything happens on a single sheet pan: broccolini roasts along the
  other half until the florets char and go nutty and the stems stay snappy-sweet.
  *The trick is the timing stagger — the broccolini comes out at twelve minutes
  while the salmon goes back for five more, so each finishes exactly right instead
  of one waiting on the other.* Pull the fish at 135–145°F and it stays silky.

ingredients:
  - section: Salmon & rub
    items:
      - 2 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each), skin-on
      - 2 tsp olive oil
      - 1 tbsp brown sugar
      - 1 tbsp paprika
      - "¾ tsp kosher salt"
      - "½ tsp freshly ground black pepper"
      - "½ tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)"
  - section: Broccolini
    items:
      - 1 bunch broccolini (about 8 oz), trimmed
      - 1 tbsp olive oil
      - "¼ tsp kosher salt"
      - "¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper"
      - "¼ tsp red pepper flakes"
steps:
  - section: Prep
    items:
      - Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with parchment or foil.
      - Pat the salmon very dry. Rub each fillet all over with olive oil so the seasoning has something to grip.
      - Stir the brown sugar, paprika, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes together. Press the rub firmly onto the top of each fillet in a thick, even coat.
      - Toss the broccolini with its olive oil, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes until evenly slicked.
  - section: Roast
    items:
      - Arrange the salmon skin-side down on one half of the sheet pan and spread the broccolini in a single layer on the other half.
      - Roast for 12 minutes, then pull the pan and remove the broccolini to a plate — the tips should be charred and the stems tender-crisp.
      - Return just the salmon to the oven for about 5 more minutes, until an instant-read thermometer reads 135–145°F at the thickest part and the crust is glossy and set.
      - Let the salmon rest 2 minutes, then plate alongside the broccolini.
tips:
  - Press the rub in hard and leave the fillets uncovered while the oven heats — a drier surface lets the brown sugar caramelize into a real crust instead of steaming.
  - Pull the salmon at 135°F for silky, or 140–145°F if you like it firmer; carryover adds a few degrees during the rest, so don't chase 145 in the oven.
  - Roast the broccolini in a genuine single layer with space between the stems — crowd it and it steams pale instead of charring. Two pans beat one crowded one.
  - No broccolini? Broccoli florets, asparagus, or green beans all roast happily in the same 12-minute window.
extras:
  - label: From the garden
    note: A squeeze of lemon and a scatter of torn basil or a little minced serrano over the finished fish brightens the sweet crust.
  - label: Make it a plate
    note: Slide it onto rice, farro, or a smear of Greek yogurt whisked with lemon and garlic to catch the caramelized bits.
  - label: Dial the heat
    note: The red pepper flakes are the whole spice budget here — drop them for Lauren-mild, or double them and add a pinch of cayenne to the rub for real heat.
