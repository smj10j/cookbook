pitch: >-
  The trick is treating eggplant like a steak: cut it into thick spears, sear the
  cut faces hard until they're bronzed and collapsing, then let them drink up a
  glossy sweet-and-sour glaze of rice vinegar, tamari, orange juice, and a little
  ketchup until every piece turns lacquered and jammy. A cool bok choy-and-carrot
  slaw dressed in lime and Aleppo goes down first for crunch, the eggplant piles on
  top, and a scatter of cashews tossed with Korean chile flakes brings the heat and
  the snap. *Roll it tight in soft lavash and the whole thing eats like the best
  vegetarian burrito you've never had.*

ingredients:
  - section: Sweet-and-sour eggplant
    items:
      - 1 lb Asian eggplant (about 2), cut into ½-inch-thick spears
      - 2 tbsp neutral oil
      - 3 tbsp rice vinegar
      - 2 tbsp tamari
      - 2 tbsp orange juice
      - 1 tbsp ketchup
      - 1 tbsp brown sugar
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - section: Spiced cashews
    items:
      - ⅓ cup roasted cashews, coarsely chopped
      - ½ tsp gochugaru (Korean chile flakes)
      - Pinch of salt
  - section: Bok choy slaw
    items:
      - 2 heads baby bok choy, thinly sliced crosswise
      - 1 carrot, coarsely grated or julienned
      - ¼ cup chopped cilantro
      - 2 tbsp lime juice
      - 1 tbsp neutral oil
      - ½ tsp Aleppo pepper
      - Salt, to taste
  - section: To wrap
    items:
      - 2 lavash flatbreads (or large flour tortillas)
steps:
  - section: Glaze the eggplant
    items:
      - In a small bowl, stir together the rice vinegar, tamari, orange juice, ketchup, brown sugar, and garlic.
      - Heat the neutral oil in a large frying pan over medium-high. Add the eggplant cut-side down and cook, turning once, until deeply browned and tender, 6–8 minutes.
      - Pour in the sweet-and-sour blend and simmer, tossing to coat, until the glaze reduces and clings and the eggplant is glossy, 2–3 minutes. Remove from the heat.
  - section: Toast the cashews & make the slaw
    items:
      - In a dry small pan over medium heat, toast the cashews until fragrant, 2–3 minutes, then toss with the gochugaru and a pinch of salt.
      - In a bowl, whisk the lime juice, oil, Aleppo, and a pinch of salt. Add the bok choy, carrot, and cilantro and toss to coat.
  - section: Assemble
    items:
      - Warm the lavash briefly in a dry pan or microwave so it rolls without cracking.
      - Spoon the slaw across the lower third of each lavash, lay the glazed eggplant spears on top, and shower with the spiced cashews.
      - Roll up tightly, tucking the sides, then slice in half on the diagonal.
tips:
  - Salt the eggplant spears and let them sit 10 minutes, then pat dry — they brown faster and soak up less oil.
  - Don't crowd the pan; eggplant steams instead of browning if the pieces touch. Work in two batches if your pan is small.
  - Gochugaru is mild and fruity rather than blistering — start with ½ tsp and add more to the cashews if you want more kick.
  - Roll the wraps in parchment and halve through the paper for a tidy, hand-held lunch.
extras:
  - label: From the garden
    note: Garden cilantro goes straight into the slaw; a few torn mint or basil leaves are lovely tucked in with the eggplant.
  - label: Make it heartier
    note: Add a smear of hummus or a handful of cooked rice noodles to the wrap to turn it into a bigger meal.
  - label: Make ahead
    note: The slaw and glazed eggplant both keep a day in the fridge; toast the cashews and roll fresh so the lavash stays soft.

A Sun Basket favorite of Lauren's, reworked for the flipbook. The move that makes it
sing is reducing the sweet-and-sour glaze *in the pan* so it lacquers onto the eggplant
instead of pooling — you want each spear shiny and sticky before it goes into the wrap.
