pitch: >-
  This is what a weeknight sheet pan looks like when it moonlights as
  something special. Salmon and broccoli roast together on one pan until the
  fish flakes and the florets go browned and nutty at the tips. The showpiece
  is a chunky almond-date tapenade — toasted almonds and soft Medjool dates
  chopped with fresh mint and dill, bound in a little oil — that lands on the
  hot fish sweet, herby, and crunchy all at once. *A harissa-spiked lemon-tahini
  drizzle pulls it together, threading gentle North African heat and sesame
  richness through every bite.* Twenty-odd minutes, one pan, and a plate that
  reads Mediterranean-bright.

ingredients:
  - section: Salmon & broccoli
    items:
      - 2 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each), skin-on
      - 1 lb broccoli, cut into florets
      - 1 tbsp olive oil
      - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - section: Almond-date tapenade
    items:
      - ¼ cup roasted almonds, coarsely chopped
      - 2 Medjool dates, pitted and chopped
      - 2 tbsp fresh mint leaves, chopped
      - 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
      - 2 tsp olive oil
      - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - section: Harissa-tahini sauce
    items:
      - 3 tbsp tahini
      - 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
      - 1 tsp harissa powder (or to taste)
      - 2 tbsp warm water, to thin
      - Pinch of kosher salt
steps:
  - section: Prep
    items:
      - Heat the oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan. Pat the salmon dry.
      - Make the tapenade — stir together the chopped almonds, dates, mint, dill, and olive oil in a small bowl; season with salt and pepper.
      - Make the sauce — whisk the tahini, lemon juice, and harissa powder, thinning with warm water a splash at a time until pourable; season with a pinch of salt.
  - section: Roast
    items:
      - Toss the broccoli with the olive oil, salt, and pepper and spread over one end of the sheet pan.
      - Rub the salmon all over with a little oil, season with salt and pepper, and set skin side down on the other end of the pan.
      - Roast, turning the broccoli halfway through, until the broccoli is tender and charred at the tips and the fish is opaque and flakes easily, 8 to 10 minutes.
  - section: Serve
    items:
      - Spoon the almond-date tapenade over the salmon and drizzle with the harissa-tahini sauce.
      - Serve the broccoli alongside with the remaining sauce on the side.
tips:
  - Pull the salmon at 125–130°F for silky centers — it climbs a few degrees off the heat and firms to flaky.
  - Toast raw almonds in a dry pan until fragrant before chopping; it deepens the tapenade's nuttiness.
  - Harissa brands vary in heat — start with ½ tsp, taste the sauce, and build up so it stays a warm hum, not a burn.
  - Make the tapenade and whisk the sauce up to a day ahead; both hold in the fridge and come to room temp while the oven heats.
extras:
  - label: From the garden
    note: Fresh mint from the garden is the tapenade's backbone — pick it just before chopping so it stays bright.
